sg::JitterSeriesStats struct
#include <speedgun-ng/benchmark.hpp>

specs/043-fwq-ftq: the series record of one probe cpu: the five calibration figures of E-22, the twelve series figures of FR-007, the two spectrum keys of FR-019, and the period list of FR-018 (FR-007, FR-011, FR-019, FR-020, E-23).

Contents

  • Reference
    • Public variables

The method sets the unit of the series figures: ns for FWQ, counts for FTQ (FR-013, FR-016). The percentile fields follow the nearest-rank rule of source/harness/percentiles.cpp over the kept samples read in place, and the fold of an empty kept set leaves every field at 0 (FR-008). noisePercent is the method's noise fraction in the range 0 through 100, and a maximum count of 0 answers 100 (FR-009). seriesRuntimeNs carries the window runtime, the name that keeps the rule table free of a second reading of runtimeNs (FR-021). droppedSamples counts the records a full buffer dropped (FR-014, FR-022). resampleStepNs and spectrumPoints state the resample the transform read, and periods carries at most five closed records, strongest first (FR-017, FR-018). The record stands absent for a TSC run (FR-006).
