sg::counters::TopdownUnsupported struct
#include <speedgun-ng/counters_topdown.hpp>

The refusal a selection answers when it can answer no tree (specs/042-topdown FR-004 to FR-007, FR-040, spec D-14, data-model E-10 and E-11).

vendor is the vendor text the message names, and identity is the identity in the message's own spelling, family 6 model 85 stepping 4 for an x86 part and the MIDR for an Arm part. detail is the directory the selection read, or the Arm sentence.

The struct is data beside the one call that states its line. Nothing else carries these fields to a caller, and no caller composes the line: a second copy of that sentence in a caller would be a second source for it.

std::string sg::counters::TopdownUnsupported::message() const

The one line this refusal answers (specs/042-topdown FR-040, spec D-14, contracts/topdown-selection.md).

The kind states the reason for the two refusals that read no directory, and detail states it for the other three: the directory between quote marks for the two a table's own rows state, and the Arm sentence for an MIDR. A caller prints the text and adds nothing to it.

Precondition

Postcondition

The text is one line, it carries the topdown: tag ahead of the vendor, the identity and the reason, and it names all three (FR-040, spec D-14).
